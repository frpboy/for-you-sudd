# Personas and Stakeholders

**Project:** Our Story - Mobile Birthday Experience  
**Status:** Baseline v1.0  
**Target date:** 8 August 2026  
**Document purpose:** Keep product decisions centered on the recipient while clarifying approval ownership.

> Confirmed client facts are marked **Confirmed**. Missing personal content is marked **Client input required**. Optional ideas are marked **Enhancement**.


## Primary persona - recipient

Uses a phone, may open the link in WhatsApp/Instagram/in-app browser, expects no setup, may have sound muted, may deny permissions, and may be on mobile data. The journey must remain emotionally coherent even when some media cannot play.

## Primary stakeholder - sender/client

Owns the relationship facts and emotional intent. Must approve names, dates, answers, letter, media, music rights, access policy, and retention/deletion. The sender is not expected to edit code.

## Delivery stakeholder - project owner

Owns scope, acceptance, privacy, repository, domain, deployment, and agent coordination. Decides what to cut when quality or deadline is threatened.

## Implementers - AI agents

Operate under narrow task contracts. They inspect before editing, do not invent content, do not add dependencies without justification, and cannot declare release readiness.

## Test reviewer

Uses supported real devices and an unauthenticated browser. Has authority to block release for privacy, crash, navigation, media, copy, or accessibility defects.
