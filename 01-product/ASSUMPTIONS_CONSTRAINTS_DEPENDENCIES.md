# Assumptions, Constraints, and Dependencies

**Project:** Our Story - Mobile Birthday Experience  
**Status:** Baseline v1.0  
**Target date:** 8 August 2026  
**Document purpose:** Make planning premises explicit so agents do not silently improvise.

> Confirmed client facts are marked **Confirmed**. Missing personal content is marked **Client input required**. Optional ideas are marked **Enhancement**.


## Assumptions

- The recipient primarily uses a modern phone browser.
- The client can approve content quickly.
- A private hosting/deployment account and domain can be configured.
- Media can be legally used for this private experience.
- The site is a finite campaign, not a permanent product.

## Constraints

- Event date: 8 August 2026.
- Personal names, media, letter, domain, and music are not yet finalized in the supplied source.
- Browser autoplay and sensor support vary.
- Many personal assets create significant transfer and privacy risk.
- AI agents may work concurrently and need strict file/task ownership.

## Dependencies

- Client approvals and asset delivery.
- DNS and hosting ownership.
- Access-control option compatible with the hosting plan.
- Real-device test access.
- Rights-cleared music and consent for media subjects.
