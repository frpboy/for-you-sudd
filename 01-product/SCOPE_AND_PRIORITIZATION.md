# Scope and Prioritization

**Project:** Our Story - Mobile Birthday Experience  
**Status:** Baseline v1.0  
**Target date:** 8 August 2026  
**Document purpose:** Prevent deadline-driven scope expansion and define cut order.

> Confirmed client facts are marked **Confirmed**. Missing personal content is marked **Client input required**. Optional ideas are marked **Enhancement**.


## MoSCoW release boundary

### Must

All P0 items in the PRD, privacy protection, accurate content, complete mobile navigation, audio coordination, real-device QA, deployment verification, rollback, and deletion plan.

### Should

Memory jar, reasons, future dreams, tap cake, replay, and modest confetti after P0 is green.

### Could

Secret memories, scratch card, one lightweight easter egg, wake lock, and multiple soundtrack chapters.

### Won’t for this release unless separately approved

CMS, database, user accounts, public comments, native app, heavy 3D, commercial analytics, permanent hosting promise, or biometric/device identity.

## Cut order under pressure

1. Remove 3D and sensor features.
2. Reduce soundtrack to one rights-cleared track.
3. Use tap-based cake/gift only.
4. Defer secret gallery and scratch card.
5. Reduce optional memory counts.
6. Never cut access protection, content approval, error recovery, accessibility, or production QA.
