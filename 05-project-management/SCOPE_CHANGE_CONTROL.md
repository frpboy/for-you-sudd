# Scope Change Control

**Project:** Our Story - Mobile Birthday Experience  
**Status:** Baseline v1.0  
**Target date:** 8 August 2026  
**Document purpose:** Control late additions without destabilizing the birthday release.

> Confirmed client facts are marked **Confirmed**. Missing personal content is marked **Client input required**. Optional ideas are marked **Enhancement**.


A change request records requester, date, description, reason, priority, affected requirements/screens/assets, architecture/dependency impact, test impact, privacy/rights impact, estimate, cut proposal, and decision.

After 6 August 18:00 IST, approve only critical defect fixes, fact corrections, access/privacy fixes, or a small content replacement with bounded regression. New effects, albums, tracks, sensor features, or structural flow changes are deferred.

An approved addition must identify an equal or greater complexity removal unless schedule buffer remains demonstrably intact.
