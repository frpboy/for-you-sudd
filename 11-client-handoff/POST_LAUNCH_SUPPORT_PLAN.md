# Post-Launch Support Plan

**Project:** Our Story - Mobile Birthday Experience  
**Status:** Baseline v1.0  
**Target date:** 8 August 2026  
**Document purpose:** Define controlled support during the celebration window.

> Confirmed client facts are marked **Confirmed**. Missing personal content is marked **Client input required**. Optional ideas are marked **Enhancement**.


## Support window

Define exact start/end in IST. During the no-change window, only S0/S1 defects, factual corrections, or access failures are changed. All other improvements are deferred.

## Triage

S0 private exposure -> disable immediately. S1 cannot access/complete -> rollback or fix. S2 major media issue -> use skip/fallback or targeted replacement. S3 visual polish -> defer unless risk-free.

## Communication

Use one owner channel. Include build/deployment ID, device/browser, safe reproduction, and whether rollback is needed. Never send access secrets or personal screenshots through unapproved channels.
