# Operations Runbook

**Project:** Our Story - Mobile Birthday Experience  
**Status:** Baseline v1.0  
**Target date:** 8 August 2026  
**Document purpose:** Provide routine and emergency operational procedures for the live experience.

> Confirmed client facts are marked **Confirmed**. Missing personal content is marked **Client input required**. Optional ideas are marked **Enhancement**.


## Routine checks

Open production from unauthenticated and authenticated clean browsers, confirm TLS/domain, access, first screen, one photo/video/audio item, final section, and no unexpected indexing/social preview. Check hosting status without inspecting personal content unnecessarily.

## Safe content replacement

Obtain approval, update inventory/config, produce derivative/checksum, run validation/tests, create protected preview, client approve exact change, deploy immutably, verify, retain previous rollback.

## Emergency disable

Remove production alias or enable provider protection, rotate secrets, and communicate through the private owner channel. Do not troubleshoot by making the deployment public.

## Ownership contacts

Fill hosting, domain, repository, storage, project owner, privacy incident, and technical support contacts before launch.
