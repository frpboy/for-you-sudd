# Your Private Birthday Story

This website is a private birthday keepsake made for Sudd. It is designed to feel personal and calm on a phone, with photographs, short moving memories, voice notes, questions, letters, and a final surprise. The experience is protected by a passphrase, so only people who have been given access can open it.

The story is best enjoyed on a mobile phone with a stable internet connection. Open the private link in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone, enter the passphrase, and turn the phone sound on if you would like to hear the ambient music and voice notes. Some phones do not allow music to begin until the first touch; if that happens, the music button in the top-right corner starts or pauses it.

To move through the story, swipe left or right, or gently tap near the outer edge of the screen. The middle of the screen is kept free for photos, videos, quiz answers, and other controls. The small bars at the top show where you are in the journey. If the browser Back button is pressed by accident, the website asks whether you want to leave instead of immediately closing the story.

Photos open when tapped. Inside a photo, you can swipe through the album, double tap to zoom, pinch to zoom with two fingers, and drag the image while it is zoomed. Tap the close button when you are ready to return to the story.

The video section is intentionally quiet and simple. The short clips play automatically as they come into view and pause when you scroll away. They are muted so the background music can continue naturally. There are no video controls to press because the clips are meant to feel like small moving photographs.

The voice-note sections can be played and paused with their own buttons. While a voice note is playing, the background music becomes softer and returns afterward. If the app or browser is placed in the background, the active voice note and visible video pause. When you return, they continue from where they stopped whenever the browser allows it.

At the end of the story, the website records the completion time only in the browser on that device. The next time the private link is opened on the same device and browser, it shows a Welcome back message. You can choose Replay to begin again from the welcome screen, or Continue to return to the saved place. This completion record is local to the device and is not shared publicly.

The website uses the existing warm beige and green design throughout the browser where the phone supports it. Dark moments, such as the opening, final screen, and photo viewer, also extend into the mobile browser chrome when supported. This is a visual touch only and does not collect extra information.

The story and its media are private. Please do not share the private link, passphrase, screenshots of the passphrase, quiz answers, or personal media without permission. If a photograph, video, voice note, caption, or question needs to be changed, contact the project owner rather than editing files directly. Updates should be made carefully so the private access and media protection remain in place.

This is a keepsake, not a public social-media page. It should be kept on a private link, with access given only to the intended recipient and trusted people. Rashi made this little journey with love.
