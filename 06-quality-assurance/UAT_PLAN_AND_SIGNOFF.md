# User Acceptance Test Plan and Sign-off

**Project:** Our Story - Mobile Birthday Experience  
**Status:** Baseline v1.0  
**Target date:** 8 August 2026  
**Document purpose:** Guide client review of personal content and the production-like experience.

> Confirmed client facts are marked **Confirmed**. Missing personal content is marked **Client input required**. Optional ideas are marked **Enhancement**.


## UAT sequence

1. Access privacy and correct recipient naming.
2. Greeting, birthday state, and story order.
3. Every date, time, location, quiz question/answer, caption, reason, memory, dream, and letter line.
4. Photo crop/order/duplicates and sensitive background details.
5. Video/voice playback and music choice/volume/rights.
6. Finale wording, animation restraint, replay.
7. Phone usability, sound-off use, and error recovery.
8. Domain, noindex/generic preview, retention/deletion date.
