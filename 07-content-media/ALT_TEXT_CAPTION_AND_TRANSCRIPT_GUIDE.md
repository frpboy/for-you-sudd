# Alt Text, Captions, and Transcript Guide

**Project:** Our Story - Mobile Birthday Experience  
**Status:** Baseline v1.0  
**Target date:** 8 August 2026  
**Document purpose:** Make personal media understandable and accessible without overexposing details.

> Confirmed client facts are marked **Confirmed**. Missing personal content is marked **Client input required**. Optional ideas are marked **Enhancement**.


## Alt text

Describe the purpose of the image in its chapter, not every visual detail. Example pattern: “The couple smiling together outside Krishnarajapuram Railway Station during their first meeting.” Use empty alt for purely decorative duplicates.

Do not include unapproved full names, exact private addresses, phone numbers, or sensitive background details. If a visible detail should remain private, crop/redact the derivative rather than hiding it only from alt text.

## Captions

Captions may provide date, location, or emotional context. They require client approval and should not duplicate nearby copy word-for-word.

## Video captions

Caption meaningful speech and important sound when relevant. Keep timing accurate and review names/language. A concise text summary may supplement but not always replace captions.

## Voice-note transcript

Provide a reviewed transcript or equivalent visible message. Mark language and uncertain words for human correction; do not auto-publish raw transcription without review.
