# Media Intake and Approval Guide

**Project:** Our Story - Mobile Birthday Experience  
**Status:** Baseline v1.0  
**Target date:** 8 August 2026  
**Document purpose:** Give the client a precise process for delivering and approving personal media.

> Confirmed client facts are marked **Confirmed**. Missing personal content is marked **Client input required**. Optional ideas are marked **Enhancement**.


## Folder delivery

Provide originals in folders such as `first-meet`, `bangalore`, `selfies`, `favourites`, `videos`, `voice`, and `music`. Do not send repeated compressed copies across messaging apps when originals are available.

## For every file

State approximate date, album/chapter, people shown, caption idea, crop preference, order, sensitive background details, whether other people consent, and whether the client owns/has permission to use it.

## Review stages

1. Intake confirmed and checksum recorded.
2. Duplicate/quality/sensitivity review.
3. Proposed web crop/derivative/poster.
4. Caption/alt/transcript review.
5. Client approval.
6. Protected preview approval.
7. Production manifest lock.

## Rejection examples

Unclear identity/consent, accidental personal documents/screens visible, location metadata concern, extremely low resolution, duplicate, unapproved intimate content, copyright uncertainty, or content that the recipient may reasonably not want published online.
