# Love Letter Content Brief

**Project:** Our Story - Mobile Birthday Experience  
**Status:** Baseline v1.0  
**Target date:** 8 August 2026  
**Document purpose:** Collect authentic material for the final letter without manufacturing personal sentiment.

> Confirmed client facts are marked **Confirmed**. Missing personal content is marked **Client input required**. Optional ideas are marked **Enhancement**.


## Inputs to collect

- How they first started talking.
- What changed after 19 December 2025.
- Meaning of 26 December 2025 and 6 January 2026.
- A specific memory at/around the railway station.
- Qualities the sender genuinely appreciates.
- What “spending time after fights” means to them.
- Birthday wishes for the coming year.
- Shared future hopes, including Switzerland if genuine.
- Preferred nickname, phrases, language mix, and signature.
- Sensitive topics or promises to avoid.

## Recommended structure

1. Direct salutation.
2. One concrete origin memory.
3. Gratitude tied to specific behaviors.
4. Acknowledgment of growth/challenges without oversharing.
5. Birthday wish focused on the recipient.
6. Hope for future memories.
7. Simple authentic close and signature.

## Quality rules

Prefer concrete details over generic superlatives. Keep the full letter readable in 1-3 minutes. Do not make legal, financial, marriage, or forever-type promises unless explicitly supplied and approved. Final text is client-owned and reviewed verbatim.
